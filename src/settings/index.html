Apple Music lyrics source order

Lyrics source priority AMLL is tried first by default, then LunaBeat, AmLyrics, and Apple Music's native lyrics. Apple Music always stays last.

Local lyrics

Import TTML from the song player
  1. Open the current Apple Music song in the fullscreen lyrics player.
  2. Open the player menu and choose Import TTML.
  3. The file is saved for that song and filled in automatically on later loads.

Local TTML takes priority for the matching song. Delete it here to return to automatic sources.

Aggressive keeps lyric and translation results for 24 hours; Moderate keeps them for 12 hours. With caching off, results are not stored, while provider indexes may still refresh when online. Clearing caches removes results only: local TTML, lyric offsets, source preferences and fonts are kept. The sizes above do not include provider indexes.
